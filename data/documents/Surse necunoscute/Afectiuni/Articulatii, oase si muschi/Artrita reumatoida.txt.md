Prevention is better than cure! A disciplined balance diet will definitely help to overcome RA effectively. Avoid Night-shade foodNightshade family vegetables are rich in alkaloid which affects the muscles, nerves and directly impacts the joints of the bones. Nightshade food includes vegetables and spices like Potatoes, Tomatoes, eggplant, hot and sweet pepper etc. Switch to Vegetarian foodSaturated fats are not a friend for RA patients definitely. Saturated fats are rich in meat, poultry and other non-vegetarian food. Plant food is packed with bioflavonoids which are anti-inflammatory. Hence it is worthwhile to be a vegetarian and also to cut on creams and other fried items to manage the pain. Green TeaArthritis foundation has conducted a research and found out that Green Tea which has plentiful of polyphenol compounds helps to reduce the intensity of swelling. Anti-toxicantSome diets really promise in reducing the setback of RA. Few of it comprise of leafy vegetables and fruits which are abundant with anti-toxicant. Omega Oilswhile omega 3- fatty acid helps to cut the inflammation, omega 6 reacts other way around. Hence anti-inflammatory agent rich food needs to be incorporated like cold water fish, flaxseed, and soybean products. On the controversy almond, walnut, sunflower seeds, corn oil are rich in Omega 6 and needs to be out of RA patient’s diet. Allergic FoodIt is equally important to concentrate on what not to eat while we speak about what to eat. Citrus fruits might increase inflammation. Sugar, salt would likewise aggravate inflammation. CalciumRA patients are prone to bone loss, hence calcium and vitamin D supplemented through food and sunlight proves helpful. Having spoken about the diet for RA, let’s have a look at the various treatments for Rheumatoid Arthritis (RA)available.To improve the flexibility in joints regular exercise, yoga, aerobics are suggested without giving much strain to the tissues. This helps in soothing the muscles and joints that are stiff and helps to reduce the pain. Drugs that would help to reduce the aggravation of RA is disease-modifying antirheumatic drug (DMARDS) As mentioned earlier since it is an autoimmune disease these help to suppress the immune system to reduce the inflammation. It is always worthier to treat the RA earlier since this disease is progressive and chronic. Substantial damage to the tissues occur in the first couple of years, hence identifying and treating it at the premature stage would delay the progress and decrease the pain.

Number One Golden Paste (GP) recipe that has been tried and tested by thousands of TUG users across the globe.

This recipe may be used for humans as well as animals. Ingredients:

### 1/2 cup (125 mls/60gms) turmeric powder

1 cup water (250 mls) PLUS 1 cup water in reserve, if needed 1/3 cup (70 mls) one of the following oils: Raw (unrefined) Cold Pressed Coconut Oil, Linseed (flaxseed) oil or Virgin/Extra Virgin Olive Oil 2-3 teaspoons freshly ground black pepper Please Note: the amount of pepper added has recently been increased (May 2016) on Doug English’s recommendations.

Omit pepper if you cannot tolerate it. The absorption of turmeric will still be improved by cooking the paste AND by the inclusion of oil, BUT remember it will be less effective without the pepper. Bring the turmeric and water to a boil, then lower the heat and simmer until you have a thick paste. This should take about 7-10 minutes and you may need to add additional water along the way.

Add the freshly ground pepper and oil at the end of cooking, when the turmeric and water mixture has cooled down to just warm. Stir well to incorporate the oil and allow to cool.

The Golden Paste will keep for 2 weeks, refrigerated Freeze a portion if you think you have too much to use within two weeks.

Use for Golden Milk, Smoothies, stir into Yogurt, add to your dinner plate as a condiment… AND don’t forget your pet’s dinner plate or your horse feed … run wild!
