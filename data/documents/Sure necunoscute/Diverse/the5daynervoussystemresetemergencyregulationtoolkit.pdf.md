DAY 1 - THE FREEZE BREAKER
For when you shut down, dissociate, or go numb
The Sensory Shock Protocol (30 seconds)
When you freeze, your nervous system is playing dead. Words won't
wake it. Thoughts can't reach it. You need biological disruption
Step 1: Temperature Override (10 seconds)
Grab ice cubes or run cold water
Apply to wrists, behind ears, or splash on face
Your brainstem must respond to temperature - it has no choice
Step 2: Pressure Activation (10 seconds)
Squeeze your entire body tight - every muscle
Hold for 5 seconds like you're bracing for impact
Release suddenly and completely
This forces your system to recognize it HAS a body
Step 3: Vigorous Movement (10 seconds)
Shake your hands like you're flinging water off them
Shake your head NO vigorously
Stomp your feet hard 5 times
Movement proves to your nervous system that freeze is over
Why This Works: Freeze is your nervous system's oldest response -
it predates fight or flight. The only thing older? Temperature
regulation and movement. You're using evolution against trauma.
Practice this NOW, before you need it. Your nervous system learns
through repetition, not emergency.
